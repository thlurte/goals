### 🔹 Part 1: Theory & Concepts (Monday Syllabus)
* **Topic & Mathematical Foundation**: Probability Primer, Expectation, Variance & The Johnson-Lindenstrauss Lemma
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Expectation & Variance in $\mathbb{R}^d$**:
     $$\mathbb{E}[X] = \int_{-\infty}^\infty x f_X(x) dx, \quad \operatorname{Var}(X) = \mathbb{E}\left[(X - \mathbb{E}[X])^2\right] = \mathbb{E}[X^2] - (\mathbb{E}[X])^2$$
  2. **The Gaussian Distribution**:
     $$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} \exp\left( -\frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right)$$
  3. **The Johnson-Lindenstrauss (JL) Lemma**:
     * For any set of $n$ points $V \subset \mathbb{R}^D$ and error tolerance $\epsilon \in (0, 1)$, there exists a linear projection matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times D}$ with $d = \mathcal{O}\left( \frac{\ln n}{\epsilon^2} \right)$ such that for all $u, v \in V$:
       $$(1 - \epsilon) \|u - v\|_2^2 \le \left\| \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} A u - \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} A v \right\|_2^2 \le (1 + \epsilon) \|u - v\|_2^2$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove the variance transformation identity $\operatorname{Var}(aX + b) = a^2 \operatorname{Var}(X)$ from the definition of expectation.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: For $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$, prove that $\mathbb{E}[Z^2] = 1$ and $\mathbb{E}[Z^4] = 3$ using integration by parts on the Gaussian integral.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For a database of $n = 10^6$ vectors and distortion $\epsilon = 0.15$, compute the minimum target dimension $d \approx \frac{8 \ln n}{\epsilon^2}$.

### 🔹 Part 2: Properties & Geometric Symmetries (Tuesday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Calculus* — Gilbert Strang
* **Section**: §13.4 (*Tangent Planes, Linear Approximations & Total Differentials*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Tangent Plane to a Surface $z = f(x, y)$**:
     $$z - z_0 = f_x(x_0, y_0)(x - x_0) + f_y(x_0, y_0)(y - y_0) \quad \text{where } z_0 = f(x_0, y_0)$$
  2. **Multivariable Linear Approximation**:
     $$L(\mathbf{x}) = f(\mathbf{x}_0) + \nabla f(\mathbf{x}_0)^T (\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_0)$$
  3. **The Total Differential**:
     $$df = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1} dx_1 + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2} dx_2 + \dots + \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_d} dx_d = \nabla f(\mathbf{x}) \cdot d\mathbf{x}$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive the tangent plane equation using the surface normal vector $\mathbf{n} = \langle f_x(x_0, y_0), f_y(x_0, y_0), -1 \rangle$ and the orthogonality condition $\mathbf{n} \cdot (\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}_0) = 0$.
  - [ ] **Problem 2**: Compute the tangent plane and local linear approximation $L(x, y)$ for $f(x, y) = \sqrt{20 - x^2 - 7y^2}$ at $(2, 1)$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: Compute the total differential $d(\langle \mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \rangle)$ with respect to independent perturbations $d\mathbf{u}$ and $d\mathbf{v}$.

### 🔹 Part 3: Core Theorems & Derivations (Wednesday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Calculus* — Gilbert Strang
* **Section**: §13.5 (*The Multivariable Chain Rule for Paths & Surfaces*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Chain Rule for Parametric Paths (Gradient Along a Trajectory)**:
     $$\frac{dz}{dt} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\frac{dy}{dt} = \nabla f(\mathbf{r}(t)) \cdot \mathbf{r}'(t)$$
  2. **Chain Rule for Composite Multivariable Functions $z = f(u(s, t), v(s, t))$**:
     $$\frac{\partial z}{\partial s} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial u}\frac{\partial u}{\partial s} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial v}\frac{\partial v}{\partial s}, \quad \frac{\partial z}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial u}\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial v}\frac{\partial v}{\partial t}$$
  3. **The General Jacobian Matrix Formulation**:
     $$\mathbf{J}_{f \circ g}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{J}_f(g(\mathbf{x})) \cdot \mathbf{J}_g(\mathbf{x})$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove $\frac{dz}{dt} = \nabla f(\mathbf{r}(t)) \cdot \mathbf{r}'(t)$ by expanding $\Delta z = f_x \Delta x + f_y \Delta y + \epsilon_1 \Delta x + \epsilon_2 \Delta y$, dividing by $\Delta t$, and evaluating the limit as $\Delta t \to 0$.
  - [ ] **Problem 2**: Given $z = e^{xy} \ln(x + y)$ with $x = s \cos t$ and $y = s \sin t$, compute the partial derivatives $\frac{\partial z}{\partial s}$ and $\frac{\partial z}{\partial t}$ at $(s=2, t=0)$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: Write out the $3 \times 2$ Jacobian matrix product for $\mathbf{f}(u, v) = \langle u^2 v, u + v^3, \sin(uv) \rangle$ composed with $\mathbf{g}(s, t) = \langle s e^t, s^2 + t^2 \rangle$.
