* **5-Part Standard Structure**:
  1. **Mathematical Foundation**:
     * Spectral Graph Laplacian $L = D - W$, positive semi-definiteness $\mathbf{x}^T L \mathbf{x} \ge 0$, and the Fiedler vector $\lambda_2$.

     * Pointer jumping and heap fragmentation: Why `std::vector<std::vector<uint32_t>>` causes $80\%$ cache miss rates.
     * Flat CSR adjacency packing and zero-overhead monotonic visited-tag sets.
  3. **The Engine Architecture**:
     * `secan` HNSW design: Multi-layer CSR storage, exponential skip-list hierarchy, and Algorithm 4 diverse neighbor selection.
  4. **Empirical Benchmarks**:
     * SIFT1M & 768-D dense text Recall@10 vs QPS Pareto scaling ($>15{,}000\text{ QPS}$ at $95\%$ recall).
  5. **Key Takeaway**:
     * Graph-based vector search breaks the linear scan barrier: by structuring embedding space into navigable small-world hierarchies, query latency scales logarithmically $\mathcal{O}(\log N)$.
