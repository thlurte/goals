## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Calculus* — Gilbert Strang
* **Focus**: Multivariable Calculus Comprehensive Synthesis (Gradients, Hessians, Lagrange & Graph Laplacians)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Gradient Orthogonality Principle**:
     * The gradient $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_0)$ is strictly normal to the tangent hyperplane of the level hypersurface $f(\mathbf{x}) = c$ at $\mathbf{x}_0$.
  2. **Spectral Curvature & Positive Definiteness**:
     * The eigenvalues of the Hessian $\mathbf{H}_f(\mathbf{x}_0)$ represent the principal curvatures of the surface.
     * $\mathbf{H}_f \succ 0 \iff \lambda_{\min}(\mathbf{H}_f) > 0 \implies$ strictly convex local minimum.
  3. **The Spectral Graph Partitioning Connection**:
     $$\min_{\mathbf{x} \in \{-1, +1\}^n, \mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{1} = 0} \frac{1}{4} \mathbf{x}^T L \mathbf{x} \quad \xrightarrow{\text{continuous relaxation}} \quad \min_{\mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{x} = n, \mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{1} = 0} \mathbf{x}^T L \mathbf{x} = n \lambda_2$$
     * The discrete min-cut problem relaxes to the Fiedler eigenvalue problem on the Graph Laplacian.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that $\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_0) \cdot \mathbf{r}'(0) = 0$ for any curve $\mathbf{r}(t)$ lying on the level set $f(\mathbf{x}) = c$ passing through $\mathbf{x}_0$ at $t=0$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that a quadratic form $Q(\mathbf{h}) = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{h}^T \mathbf{H} \mathbf{h} > 0$ for all $\mathbf{h} \ne \mathbf{0}$ if and only if all eigenvalues of symmetric matrix $\mathbf{H}$ are strictly positive.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: Write out the complete Lagrangian for the continuous spectral bisection problem: $\mathcal{L}(\mathbf{x}, \lambda, \mu) = \mathbf{x}^T L \mathbf{x} - \lambda(\mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{x} - 1) - \mu(\mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{1})$, deriving $L \mathbf{x} = \lambda \mathbf{x}$.
