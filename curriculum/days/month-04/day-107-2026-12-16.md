## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Probability* — Dimitri P. Bertsekas & John N. Tsitsiklis
* **Sections**: §7.3–7.4 (*The Weak Law of Large Numbers (WLLN) & Convergence in Probability*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Sample Mean of i.i.d. Random Variables**:
     * Let $X_1, \dots, X_n$ be i.i.d. with mean $\mu$ and finite variance $\sigma^2$.
     * Sample Mean: $\bar{X}_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n X_i \implies E[\bar{X}_n] = \mu, \operatorname{Var}(\bar{X}_n) = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}$.
  2. **The Weak Law of Large Numbers (WLLN)**:
     * For any arbitrary tolerance $\epsilon > 0$:
       $$\lim_{n \to \infty} P\left( |\bar{X}_n - \mu| \ge \epsilon \right) = 0$$
     * **Chebyshev Proof**: $P\left(|\bar{X}_n - \mu| \ge \epsilon\right) \le \frac{\operatorname{Var}(\bar{X}_n)}{\epsilon^2} = \frac{\sigma^2}{n \epsilon^2} \xrightarrow{n \to \infty} 0$.
  3. **Convergence in Probability**:
     * A sequence of random variables $Y_n$ converges in probability to $c$ ($Y_n \xrightarrow{p} c$) $\iff \forall \epsilon > 0, \lim_{n \to \infty} P(|Y_n - c| \ge \epsilon) = 0$.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive the WLLN rigorously by applying Chebyshev's inequality to $\bar{X}_n$ with variance $\frac{\sigma^2}{n}$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove the Continuous Mapping Theorem: If $Y_n \xrightarrow{p} c$ and function $g$ is continuous at $c$, then $g(Y_n) \xrightarrow{p} g(c)$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: In an empirical vector search benchmark, we estimate mean query latency $\mu$ by taking the average $\bar{T}_n$ over $n$ independent queries. ## 📖 07:30 – 08:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Book / Paper**: *Billion-Scale Similarity Search with GPUs* (Faiss GPU) §4–5 (*2-Stage Coarse Routing & Warp-Cooperative List Scanning*)
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Two-Stage Search Pipeline**:
     * **Stage 1 (Coarse Centroid Routing)**: Batched GEMM computes distance between Query and all $C = 4{,}096$ coarse centroids; in-register Bitonic selection extracts top-$nprobe$ ($nprobe = 32$) list IDs.
     * **Stage 2 (Warp-Cooperative List Scanning)**: Warps are assigned to active inverted lists. Each warp reads the list offset from `d_list_offsets` and scans the contiguous code segment in GMEM with coalesced 128-byte reads.
  2. **Inter-List Load Balancing**:
     * Inverted lists have skewed lengths. Distributing lists across thread blocks via atomic work queues prevents warp stragglers.
