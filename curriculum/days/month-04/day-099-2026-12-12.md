### 🔹 Part 1: Theory & Concepts (Monday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Probability* — Dimitri P. Bertsekas & John N. Tsitsiklis
* **Sections**: §5.1–5.3 (*Continuous Random Variables, Probability Density Functions (PDF) & CDF Properties*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Continuous Random Variables & Probability Density Functions (PDF)**:
     * A random variable $X$ is continuous if there exists a non-negative function $f_X(x)$ such that:
       $$P(a \le X \le b) = \int_a^b f_X(x) dx \quad \text{with } \int_{-\infty}^\infty f_X(x) dx = 1$$
     * Single-point probability is strictly zero: $P(X = c) = 0$ for all $c \in \mathbb{R}$.
  2. **Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF)**:
     * $F_X(x) = P(X \le x) = \int_{-\infty}^x f_X(t) dt$.
     * Fundamental Theorem of Calculus: $\frac{d}{dx} F_X(x) = f_X(x)$ (at all continuity points).
  3. **Continuous Expectation, LOTUS & Uniform Distribution**:
     * $E[X] = \int_{-\infty}^\infty x f_X(x) dx, \quad \operatorname{Var}(X) = \int_{-\infty}^\infty (x - \mu)^2 f_X(x) dx$.
     * $X \sim \mathcal{U}(a, b)$: $f_X(x) = \frac{1}{b-a} \implies E[X] = \frac{a+b}{2}, \operatorname{Var}(X) = \frac{(b-a)^2}{12}$.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove $\operatorname{Var}(X) = \frac{(b-a)^2}{12}$ for $X \sim \mathcal{U}(a, b)$ by evaluating the integral $\int_a^b x^2 \frac{1}{b-a}dx - \left(\frac{a+b}{2}\right)^2$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove the continuous tail-sum expectation formula for non-negative random variables: $E[X] = \int_0^\infty (1 - F_X(x))dx$ using integration by parts on $\int_0^\infty x f_X(x)dx$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: In vector quantization, let scalar rounding error $\epsilon$ be distributed uniformly on $[-\Delta/2, \Delta/2]$. Compute $E[\epsilon]$, $\operatorname{Var}(\epsilon) = \frac{\Delta^2}{12}$, and the signal-to-quantization-noise ratio (SQNR).

### 🔹 Part 2: Properties & Geometric Symmetries (Tuesday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Probability* — Dimitri P. Bertsekas & John N. Tsitsiklis
* **Sections**: §5.4–5.5 (*Exponential Distribution, Memoryless Property & The Normal / Gaussian $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Exponential Distribution $\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)$**:
     * $f_X(x) = \lambda e^{-\lambda x}$ ($x \ge 0$) $\implies F_X(x) = 1 - e^{-\lambda x}$.
     * **Mean & Variance**: $E[X] = \frac{1}{\lambda}$, $\operatorname{Var}(X) = \frac{1}{\lambda^2}$.
     * **Continuous Memoryless Property**: For all $s, t > 0$:
       $$P(X > s + t \mid X > s) = \frac{e^{-\lambda(s+t)}}{e^{-\lambda s}} = e^{-\lambda t} = P(X > t)$$
  2. **The Normal (Gaussian) Distribution $\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$**:
     $$f_X(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} \exp\left( -\frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right)$$
     * **Standardization**: If $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, then $Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$.
     * **Affine Invariance**: $aX + b \sim \mathcal{N}(a\mu + b, a^2\sigma^2)$.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove the continuous memoryless property of the Exponential distribution and prove that if a continuous positive variable satisfies $P(X > s+t \mid X > s) = P(X > t)$, its survival function must be $G(t) = e^{-\lambda t}$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove $E[Z] = 0$ and $E[Z^2] = 1$ for standard normal $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ using odd function symmetry and integration by parts on $\int_{-\infty}^\infty z (z e^{-z^2/2})dz$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: Let $T_1, \dots, T_k$ represent independent SSD retrieval latencies for $k$ parallel shards where $T_i \sim \operatorname{Exp}(\lambda_i)$. Prove that the fastest shard response $\min(T_1, \dots, T_k) \sim \operatorname{Exp}(\sum_{i=1}^k \lambda_i)$ and that $P(T_1 = \min(T_i)) = \frac{\lambda_1}{\sum \lambda_i}$.

### 🔹 Part 3: Core Theorems & Derivations (Wednesday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Probability* — Dimitri P. Bertsekas & John N. Tsitsiklis
* **Sections**: §6.1–6.3 (*Moments & Moment Generating Functions (MGF)*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Moment Generating Functions (MGF)**:
     * Definition: $M_X(t) = E\left[e^{tX}\right]$ (for $t$ in an open neighborhood around 0).
     * **Moment Generation via Derivatives**:
       $$E[X^n] = \left. \frac{d^n}{dt^n} M_X(t) \right|_{t=0}$$
  2. **MGF of Independent Sums & Gaussian Distribution**:
     * If $X, Y$ are independent, $M_{X+Y}(t) = E[e^{t(X+Y)}] = E[e^{tX}] E[e^{tY}] = M_X(t) M_Y(t)$.
     * **Standard Normal**: $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1) \implies M_Z(t) = e^{t^2/2}$.
     * **General Normal**: $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2) \implies M_X(t) = \exp\left( \mu t + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 t^2 \right)$.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive $M_Z(t) = e^{t^2/2}$ for $Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ by completing the square in the exponent: $\int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{tz - z^2/2}dz = e^{t^2/2} \int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-(z-t)^2/2}dz = \sqrt{2\pi} e^{t^2/2}$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Differentiate $M_Z(t) = e^{t^2/2}$ four times to derive the standard Gaussian moments: $E[Z] = 0, E[Z^2] = 1, E[Z^3] = 0, E[Z^4] = 3$ (proving Gaussian excess kurtosis is exactly 0).
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: In high-dimensional dot product retrieval $S = \sum_{i=1}^d Q_i K_i$ where $Q_i, K_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1/\sqrt{d})$ are i.i.d., compute the MGF $M_S(t)$ and show that $S \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ as $d \to \infty$.
