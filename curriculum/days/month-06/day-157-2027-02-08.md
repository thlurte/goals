* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **ARM NEON Register Architecture**:
     * Provides 32 $\times$ 128-bit vector registers (`v0`–`v31`).
     * Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and AWS Graviton 3/4 feature **4 independent 128-bit NEON execution pipelines**, retiring 4 vector FMAs per clock cycle ($16\text{ FP32 FLOPs/cycle}$).
  2. **Core NEON Intrinsics for Vector Search**:
     * `vld1q_f32(ptr)`: Load 4 packed 32-bit floats.
     * `vfmaq_f32(acc, a, b)`: Fused Multiply-Accumulate ($acc \leftarrow acc + a \times b$).
     * `vaddvq_f32(acc)`: Fast horizontal lane reduction summing 4 float components into scalar.
