### 🔹 Part 1: Advanced Theorems & Derivations (Thursday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Spectral Graph Theory* — Daniel A. Spielman / Fan Chung
* **Sections**: §2.4 (*Cheeger's Inequality: Rigorous Proof via Fiedler Sweep Cuts*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Fiedler Vector & Sweep-Cut Algorithm**:
     * Let $\mathbf{v}_2$ be the eigenvector for $\lambda_2(\mathcal{L})$ and define $\mathbf{y} = D^{-1/2}\mathbf{v}_2$.
     * Sort vertices such that $y_{\pi(1)} \le y_{\pi(2)} \le \dots \le y_{\pi(n)}$.
     * For each cut $S_k = \{\pi(1), \dots, \pi(k)\}$, compute conductance $\phi(S_k)$.
  2. **Cheeger's Inequality Upper Bound**:
     $$\min_{1 \le k < n} \phi(S_k) \le \sqrt{2 \lambda_2}$$
     * **Full Cheeger Theorem**:
       $$\frac{\lambda_2}{2} \le \phi(G) \le \min_k \phi(S_k) \le \sqrt{2 \lambda_2}$$
     * Provides a constructive $\mathcal{O}(|E| + n \log n)$ approximation algorithm for the NP-hard minimum graph conductance problem.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for the sweep cut numerator: Show that $\sum_{(u, v) \in E} |y_u^2 - y_v^2| \le \sqrt{\sum_{(u, v) \in E} (y_u - y_v)^2} \sqrt{\sum_{(u, v) \in E} (y_u + y_v)^2} \le \sqrt{\lambda_2} \sqrt{2 \sum_u d_u y_u^2}$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that integrating cut sizes over threshold $t \in [y_{\pi(1)}, y_{\pi(n)}]$ satisfies $\int |\partial S(t)| dt = \sum_{(u, v) \in E} |y_u - y_v|$ and $\int \operatorname{vol}(S(t)) dt = \sum_u d_u |y_u|$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: On a 6-node barbell graph $K_3 - K_3$ joined by edge $(3, 4)$, compute the Fiedler vector coordinates $\mathbf{y} = [-a, -a, -b, +b, +a, +a]^T$ and demonstrate that the sweep cut at $k = 3$ isolates the exact bridge edge with conductance $\phi(S_3) = 1/7$.

### 🔹 Part 2: Core Proofs & Problem Sets (Friday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Spectral Graph Theory* — Daniel A. Spielman / Fan Chung
* **Sections**: §3.1 (*Expander Graphs, The Expander Mixing Lemma & Ramanujan Graphs*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **$d$-Regular Spectral Expansion**:
     * For a $d$-regular graph, eigenvalues of adjacency matrix $A$ are $d = \mu_1 \ge \mu_2 \ge \dots \ge \mu_n \ge -d$.
     * Spectral gap: $\gamma = d - \mu_2$.
  2. **The Expander Mixing Lemma**:
     * For any two subsets of vertices $S, T \subseteq V$:
       $$\left| |E(S, T)| - \frac{d |S| |T|}{n} \right| \le \mu_2 \sqrt{|S| |T| \left( 1 - \frac{|S|}{n} \right) \left( 1 - \frac{|T|}{n} \right)}$$
     * **Significance**: As $\mu_2 \to 0$, the edge distribution between any two sets converges to that of a purely random Erdős–Rényi graph.
  3. **The Alon-Boppana Bound & Ramanujan Graphs**:
     * **Alon-Boppana Theorem**: For any family of $d$-regular graphs: $\liminf_{n \to \infty} \mu_2(A) \ge 2\sqrt{d - 1}$.
     * **Ramanujan Graphs**: Graphs achieving $\mu_2 \le 2\sqrt{d - 1}$, providing optimal small-world routing with logarithmic diameter $\mathcal{O}(\log n)$.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove the Expander Mixing Lemma: Express indicator vectors $\mathbf{1}_S$ and $\mathbf{1}_T$ as linear combinations of the orthonormal eigenvectors of $A$, separate the top eigenvector $\mathbf{v}_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}\mathbf{1}$, and bound the remaining inner product by $\mu_2 \|\mathbf{1}_S\|_2 \|\mathbf{1}_T\|_2$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that the diameter of a $d$-regular Ramanujan graph is bounded by $\operatorname{diam}(G) \le \frac{\log(n - 1)}{\log(d / \mu_2)} + 2 \le \frac{2 \log n}{\log(d / 4)}$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For a 32-regular HNSW graph layer ($d = 32$), compute the Ramanujan bound $\mu_2 \le 2\sqrt{31} \approx 11.135$ and show that the graph mixing rate is $\frac{\mu_2}{d} \approx 0.348$, guaranteeing that beam search traverses $10^6$ nodes in $\le 14$ hops.
