* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Hybrid Edge Allocation**:
     * Standard HNSW edge capacity $M = 32$.
     * ACORN partitions edges per node:
       * **Spatial Edges ($M_{\text{spatial}} = 16$)**: 1-hop metric nearest neighbors for unconstrained exploration.
       * **Predicate Edges ($M_{\text{predicate}} = 16$)**: 2-hop neighbors sharing identical metadata attributes.
  2. **Zero-Overhead Predicate Routing**:
     * Nodes violating the predicate are not evaluated for candidate distance heaps, but their outgoing edges are still traversed as structural bridges, preventing search truncation.
