### 🔹 Part 1: Advanced Theorems & Derivations (Thursday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Elements of Information Theory* — Thomas M. Cover & Joy A. Thomas
* **Sections**: §2.1–2.6 (*Cross-Entropy Decomposition, KL Divergence & InfoNCE Mutual Information*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Cross-Entropy Decomposition Theorem**:
     $$H(P, Q) = -\sum_{x \in \mathcal{X}} P(x) \ln Q(x) = H(P) + D_{KL}(P \parallel Q)$$
     * Since $D_{KL}(P \parallel Q) \ge 0$ (Gibbs' inequality), $H(P, Q) \ge H(P)$ with equality iff $P = Q$.
  2. **InfoNCE as a Mutual Information Lower Bound**:
     $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = -\mathbb{E}\left[ \ln \frac{e^{\mathbf{q}^T \mathbf{k}^+ / \tau}}{e^{\mathbf{q}^T \mathbf{k}^+ / \tau} + \sum_{j=1}^K e^{\mathbf{q}^T \mathbf{k}_j^- / \tau}} \right]$$
     * **Theorem**: $I(Q; K^+) \ge \ln(K + 1) - \mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}}$.
  3. **Alignment & Uniformity Asymptotics**:
     * As negative samples $K \to \infty$, minimizing InfoNCE asymptotically optimizes:
       * **Alignment**: $\mathbb{E}[\|f(x) - f(x^+)\|_2^2] \to 0$.
       * **Uniformity**: Representations distribute uniformly on the unit sphere $\mathcal{S}^{d-1}$ maximizing entropy.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive $H(P, Q) = H(P) + D_{KL}(P \parallel Q)$ by writing $\ln Q(x) = \ln P(x) + \ln \frac{Q(x)}{P(x)}$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the Mutual Information bound $I(X; Y) \ge \ln(K + 1) - \mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}}$ using the optimal critic $f^*(x, y) = \ln \frac{p(x, y)}{p(x)p(y)}$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For temperature $\tau = 0.05$ and batch negative count $K = 32{,}768$, compute the theoretical maximum mutual information capacity $\ln(32769) \approx 10.397\text{ nats} \approx 15.0\text{ bits}$.

### 🔹 Part 2: Core Proofs & Problem Sets (Friday Syllabus)
* **Book / Topic**: *Block II Mathematical Foundations Full Synthesis* (Convex Optimization, Spectral Graphs & Information Theory)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Rayleigh-KKT Equivalence Theorem**:
     * Minimizing the graph Laplacian Rayleigh quotient subject to unit sphere and orthogonality constraints:
       $$\min_{\mathbf{x}} \mathbf{x}^T L \mathbf{x} \quad \text{subject to} \quad \mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{x} = 1, \quad \mathbf{1}^T \mathbf{x} = 0$$
     * The Lagrangian is $L(\mathbf{x}, \lambda, \nu) = \mathbf{x}^T L \mathbf{x} - \lambda (\mathbf{x}^T \mathbf{x} - 1) - \nu (\mathbf{1}^T \mathbf{x})$.
     * KKT stationarity $\nabla_{\mathbf{x}} L = 2 L \mathbf{x} - 2 \lambda \mathbf{x} - \nu \mathbf{1} = \mathbf{0}$. Multiplying by $\mathbf{1}^T$ yields $\nu = 0$, recovering the exact eigenvalue equation $L \mathbf{x} = \lambda_2 \mathbf{x}$.
  2. **Unified Geometric & Spectral Hierarchy**:
     * **Hyperplane Cuts**: Affine halfspaces $\to$ Voronoi cell convexity.
     * **Spectral Connectivity**: Second eigenvalue $\lambda_2$ bounds worst-case graph conductance $\phi(G) \ge \lambda_2 / 2$.
     * **Expander Mixing**: Small second adjacency eigenvalue $\mu_2 \le 2\sqrt{d-1}$ ensures random-like graph diameter $\mathcal{O}(\log n)$.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive the Rayleigh-KKT stationarity proof showing $L \mathbf{x} = \lambda_2 \mathbf{x}$ and $\nu = 0$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that the Cheeger inequality lower bound $\phi(G) \ge \frac{\lambda_2}{2}$ is asymptotically sharp for cycle graphs $C_n$ with $\lambda_2 \approx \frac{4\pi^2}{n^2}$ and $\phi(C_n) = \frac{4}{n}$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For a multimodal vector search index with $N = 10^6$ document pages ($1030$ tokens per page, $d=128$), verify that the end-to-end latency budget is satisfied: (a) Stage 1 ACORN coarse candidate retrieval: $<1.2\text{ ms}$, (b) Stage 2 Fused GPU MaxSim over top-200 candidates: $<150\ \mu\text{s}$, giving total query latency $<1.5\text{ ms}$.
