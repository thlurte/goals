## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Convex Optimization* — Stephen Boyd & Lieven Vandenberghe
* **Sections**: §11.4–11.5 (*The Barrier Method, Outer Centering Iterations & Complexity*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Logarithmic Barrier Method Algorithm**:
     * Given strictly feasible $x_0$, initial $t^{(0)} > 0$, barrier multiplier $\mu > 1$, and tolerance $\epsilon > 0$:
       1. **Centering Step**: Compute $x^*(t) = \arg\min_x \left( t f_0(x) + \phi(x) \right)$ subject to $A x = b$ using Newton's method.
       2. **Update**: $x \leftarrow x^*(t)$.
       3. **Stopping Criterion**: Terminate if $\frac{m}{t} < \epsilon$.
       4. **Increase Barrier Parameter**: $t \leftarrow \mu t$.
  2. **Outer Iteration Complexity Theorem**:
     $$N = \left\lceil \frac{\ln(m / (t^{(0)}\epsilon))}{\ln \mu} \right\rceil \text{ outer centering steps}$$
  3. **Self-Concordance & Inner Newton Steps**:
     * For self-concordant barriers, the total number of inner Newton steps across all outer iterations is bounded by:
       $$\text{Total Newton Steps} \le \mathcal{O}\left( \sqrt{m} \ln\left(\frac{m}{\epsilon}\right) \right)$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive the outer iteration count $N = \lceil \frac{\ln(m / (t^{(0)}\epsilon))}{\ln \mu} \rceil$ from the geometric duality gap decay $\Delta^{(k)} = \frac{m}{t^{(k)}} = \frac{m}{t^{(0)}\mu^k} \le \epsilon$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that for Linear Programs ($f_0(x) = c^T x, f_i(x) = a_i^T x - b_i$), the logarithmic barrier $\phi(x) = -\sum_{i=1}^m \ln(b_i - a_i^T x)$ is standard self-concordant.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For an optimal vector bit allocation program with $m = 128$ inequality constraints, initial $t^{(0)} = 1$, scaling factor $\mu = 10$, and error threshold $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$, compute the exact number of outer centering iterations required ($N = 9$).
