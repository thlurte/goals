* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Two-Stage Multimodal Search Architecture**:
     * **Stage 1 (Coarse Filter)**: Project $1{,}030$ visual tokens per page into a compact MUVERA Fixed-Dimensional Embedding (FDE) vector ($d = 512$); retrieve top-$200$ candidate pages via standard IVF-PQ.
     * **Stage 2 (Fine Rerank)**: Stream resident multi-vector representations of the 200 candidates to GPU; evaluate exact MaxSim in parallel.

     * Visual document embeddings are mapped from NVMe SSD via `io_uring` direct I/O into pinned host memory buffers.
