* **Book / Paper**: *GPU NN-Descent: Efficient $k$-NN Graph Construction via Warp-Level Neighbor Exchanges*
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **The NN-Descent Principle**:
     * *"A friend of my friend is likely my friend."*
     * Iteratively refine an approximate $k$-NN graph by evaluating distances between all neighbor-pairs $(v, w) \in \mathcal{N}(u) \times \mathcal{N}(u)$.
  2. **GPU Thread Block Neighborhood Joins**:
     * Assign one thread block per vertex $u$. Load current $k$-nearest neighbors into shared memory.
     * Cooperative warp threads evaluate cross-neighbor distances in parallel, updating top-$k$ heaps in registers with $>98\%$ recall in only 8–10 iterations.
