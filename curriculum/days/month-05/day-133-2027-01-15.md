## 📖 05:30 – 06:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Advanced Systems Reference**: * [Denis Bakhvalov, *Performance Analysis and Tuning on Modern CPUs*](https://book.easyperf.net/) — **Ch 10 (§10.1–10.4)** (*Microbenchmarking Pitfalls: Compiler Dead-Code Elimination & Warmup Disciplines*)
* **Book / Paper**: *CPU vs GPU Latency Crossover & PCIe Batching Overhead Analysis*
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Latency Cost Breakdown by Architecture**:
     * **CPU SIMD (AVX2)**: Latency $T_{\text{CPU}}(B) = B \cdot t_{\text{single}} + t_{\text{dispatch}}$. For $B=1$, latency is near-instantaneous ($0.06\text{ ms}$).
     * **GPU SIMT (CUDA)**: Latency $T_{\text{GPU}}(B) = t_{\text{launch}} + t_{\text{PCIe}}(B) + t_{\text{SM\_compute}}(B)$. Fixed launch overhead $\approx 0.15\text{ ms}$.
  2. **The Batching Crossover Threshold**:
     * For single-query interactive search ($B=1$), CPU wins on tail latency.
     * At batch size $B \ge 16$, GPU compute parallelism amortizes driver and bus overhead, yielding $>50\times$ throughput speedups at $B=1000$.
