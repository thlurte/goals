## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Probability* — Dimitri P. Bertsekas & John N. Tsitsiklis
* **Sections**: §12.1–12.3 (*Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) & Metropolis-Hastings Theory*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The MCMC Paradigm**:
     * Sampling from unnormalized target distribution $p(x) = \frac{\tilde{p}(x)}{Z}$ where normalizing constant $Z = \int \tilde{p}(x)dx$ is analytically intractable.
     * Construct an ergodic Markov chain whose unique stationary distribution is exactly $p(x)$.
  2. **Metropolis-Hastings Algorithm**:
     * Given current state $x$, generate candidate $y \sim q(y \mid x)$ from proposal distribution $q$.
     * Accept candidate with probability:
       $$\alpha(x, y) = \min\left( 1, \frac{p(y) q(x \mid y)}{p(x) q(y \mid x)} \right) = \min\left( 1, \frac{\tilde{p}(y) q(x \mid y)}{\tilde{p}(x) q(y \mid x)} \right)$$
     * If accepted, $X_{n+1} = y$; else $X_{n+1} = x$.
  3. **Detailed Balance Proof**:
     * For $x \ne y$, transition probability is $p(x, y) = q(y \mid x) \alpha(x, y)$.
     * $$p(x) p(x, y) = p(x) q(y \mid x) \min\left( 1, \frac{p(y) q(x \mid y)}{p(x) q(y \mid x)} \right) = \min\left( p(x) q(y \mid x), p(y) q(x \mid y) \right)$$
     * By symmetry of the $\min$ function: $p(x) p(x, y) = p(y) p(y, x)$, proving detailed balance!
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive the detailed balance identity $p(x) p(x, y) = p(y) p(y, x)$ under both cases: Case A ($\frac{p(y) q(x \mid y)}{p(x) q(y \mid x)} \le 1$) and Case B ($> 1$).
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that for symmetric proposal distributions ($q(y \mid x) = q(x \mid y)$, e.g., Gaussian random walk), the acceptance ratio simplifies to standard Metropolis rule $\alpha(x, y) = \min\left(1, \frac{p(y)}{p(x)}\right)$.
