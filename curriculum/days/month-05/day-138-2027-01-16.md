* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Parallel Scaling Efficiency Metric**:
     $$E(G) = \frac{\text{Throughput}(G)}{G \cdot \text{Throughput}(1)}$$
  2. **Amdahl's Communication Bottleneck**:
     * Scanning $N/G$ vectors takes time $T_{\text{compute}} = \frac{\mathcal{O}(N/G)}{\text{Device BW}}$.
     * Gathering top-$K$ candidates takes time $T_{\text{comm}} = (G-1) \cdot \alpha + \mathcal{O}(G \cdot K)$.
     * For large datasets ($N \ge 10\text{M}$), compute dominates communication, achieving **$>94\%$ parallel efficiency** on 4 GPUs.
