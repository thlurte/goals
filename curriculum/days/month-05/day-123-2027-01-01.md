## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Probability* — Dimitri P. Bertsekas & John N. Tsitsiklis
* **Sections**: §11.5–11.6 (*Random Walks on Graphs, Detailed Balance & Time Reversibility*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Detailed Balance Equations & Time Reversibility**:
     * A probability distribution $\boldsymbol{\pi}$ satisfies detailed balance if:
       $$\pi_i p_{ij} = \pi_j p_{ji} \quad (\forall i, j \in S)$$
     * **Theorem**: Any distribution satisfying detailed balance is guaranteed to be stationary:
       $$\sum_{i \in S} \pi_i p_{ij} = \sum_{i \in S} \pi_j p_{ji} = \pi_j \sum_{i \in S} p_{ji} = \pi_j(1) = \pi_j \implies \boldsymbol{\pi} P = \boldsymbol{\pi}$$
  2. **Random Walks on Connected Undirected Graphs**:
     * Let $G = (V, E)$ be an undirected connected graph with degree $d_i = \operatorname{deg}(i)$ and total edges $|E| = \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i \in V} d_i$.
     * Transition probability: $p_{ij} = \frac{1}{d_i}$ for $(i, j) \in E$.
     * **Stationary Distribution Theorem**:
       $$\pi_i = \frac{d_i}{2|E|}$$
       * **Proof**: $\pi_i p_{ij} = \frac{d_i}{2|E|} \cdot \frac{1}{d_i} = \frac{1}{2|E|} = \frac{d_j}{2|E|} \cdot \frac{1}{d_j} = \pi_j p_{ji}$.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that if $\pi_i p_{ij} = \pi_j p_{ji}$ holds for all pairs $(i, j)$, then $\boldsymbol{\pi}$ is stationary ($\boldsymbol{\pi} P = \boldsymbol{\pi}$).
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that for a regular graph where all vertices have degree $d_i = k$, the stationary distribution is strictly uniform $\pi_i = \frac{1}{|V|}$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: On a 5-node star graph with central hub node 1 connected to 4 perimeter leaf nodes $\{2, 3, 4, 5\}$ ($d_1 = 4, d_2=d_3=d_4=d_5=1, |E|=4$):

    * Compute the mean return time to the hub node $E[T_{11}] = \frac{1}{\pi_1} = 2.0\text{ steps}$.
