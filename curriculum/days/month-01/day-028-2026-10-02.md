## ✍️ 06:30 – 08:30 | Morning Builder Track: Technical Essay / Lab-Note Drafting (120 min)
* **Essay Number**: **Essay 04**
* **Title**: *"From Complex Rotations to RoPE and Voronoi Cells: The Geometry of Spherical Inverted Files"*
* **Target File**: `~/personal/goals/essays/essay_04.md`
* **5-Part Standard Structure**:
  1. **Mathematical Foundation**:
     * Complex rotations in 2D Euclidean planes: $z e^{i\theta} \iff \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$.
     * **Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE)** formulation: Encoding relative position $m - n$ into inner products across orthogonal 2D subspaces:
       $$\langle \mathbf{R}_{\Theta, m}^d \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{R}_{\Theta, n}^d \mathbf{k} \rangle = \operatorname{Re}\left( \sum_{i=1}^{d/2} (q_{2i-1} + i q_{2i})(k_{2i-1} - i k_{2i}) e^{i(m-n)\theta_i} \right)$$
     * Spherical Voronoi tessellation on hypersphere $\mathbb{S}^{d-1}$.
  2. **The Systems Bottleneck**:
     * Why exact search fails at scale: $10^8$ vectors $\times 128\text{ floats} \times 4\text{ B} = 51.2\text{ GB}$ (cannot be scanned in under $1\text{ second}$ on memory bus).
     * The IVF solution: Pruning $95\%\text{--}99\%$ of vector evaluations via coarse centroid routing.
  3. **The Engine Architecture**:
     * `secan` `IvfFlatIndex` design: Flat contiguous posting lists, spherical $k$-means coarse quantizer, and multi-probe query router.
  4. **Empirical Benchmarks**:
     * Pareto curve (Recall@10 vs QPS) across $K=1024$ and $\text{nprobe} \in \{1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64\}$ on SIFT1M.
  5. **Key Takeaway**:
     * Modern vector retrieval is geometric routing: by structuring high-dimensional space into Voronoi cells, we replace exhaustive memory sweeps with localized cache bursts.
