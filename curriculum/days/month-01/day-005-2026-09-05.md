## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Calculus* — Gilbert Strang
* **Sections**: §2.4–2.5 (*The Product Rule, Quotient Rule, and Trigonometric Derivatives*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Product Rule Derivation**:
     $$\frac{d}{dx}\left[u(x)v(x)\right] = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{u(x+h)v(x+h) - u(x)v(x)}{h} = u'(x)v(x) + u(x)v'(x)$$
  2. **The Quotient Rule**:
     $$\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{u(x)}{v(x)}\right] = \frac{u'(x)v(x) - u(x)v'(x)}{\left[v(x)\right]^2} \quad (v(x) \ne 0)$$
  3. **Derivatives of Trigonometric Functions**:
     $$\frac{d}{dx}\sin x = \cos x, \quad \frac{d}{dx}\cos x = -\sin x$$
     $$\frac{d}{dx}\tan x = \sec^2 x, \quad \frac{d}{dx}\sec x = \sec x \tan x$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that $\frac{d}{dx}\sin x = \cos x$ from the limit definition using the angle addition identity $\sin(x+h) = \sin x \cos h + \cos x \sin h$ and the fundamental limits $\lim_{h \to 0}\frac{\sin h}{h} = 1$ and $\lim_{h \to 0}\frac{\cos h - 1}{h} = 0$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the Quotient Rule directly by expressing $\frac{u}{v} = u \cdot v^{-1}$ and applying the Product Rule and Power Rule.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: Find the derivative of $f(x) = \frac{x^2 \sin x}{\cos x + 1}$ and compute $f'(0)$.

## 📖 07:30 – 08:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Book**: *The Art of Writing Efficient Programs* (Pikus Ch 1), CS:APP §5.14 & Hardware Profiling Playbook ([`roadmap.md#part-3`](../../roadmap.md#part-3-verification--tooling-matrix-the-hardware-profiling-playbook))
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Hardware Performance Monitoring Units (PMUs)**: Dedicated silicon registers inside CPU cores that count microarchitectural events with zero overhead.
  2. **Instructions Per Cycle (IPC)**:
     $$\text{IPC} = \frac{\text{Retired Instructions}}{\text{CPU Clock Cycles}}$$
     * Baseline scalar C++ loops typically achieve $1.0\text{--}1.5\text{ IPC}$.
     * Fully unrolled SIMD vector pipelines achieve $>3.0\text{ IPC}$.
  3. **L1 Data Cache Load Miss Rate**: Ratio of $\frac{\text{L1-dcache-load-misses}}{\text{L1-dcache-loads}}$. High miss rates indicate memory bus stalls where ALUs sit idle waiting for cache line fills from L2/L3/RAM.
  4. **Branch Misprediction Penalty**: Modern deep out-of-order execution pipelines flush 15–20 in-flight instructions upon a mispredicted branch.
