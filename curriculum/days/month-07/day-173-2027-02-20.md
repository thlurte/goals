## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book / Topic**: *Causal Masking Geometry & Lower Triangular Matrix Tiling*
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Causal Attention Masking Polyhedron**:
     * Logit mask: $M_{ij} = 0$ if $j \le i$, and $-\infty$ if $j > i$.
     * In tiled FlashAttention with block dimensions $B_r \times B_c$:
       * **Sub-diagonal Tiles** ($i B_r \ge (j+1) B_c$): Fully dense matrix multiply ($100\%$ active).
       * **Diagonal Tiles** ($i B_r < (j+1) B_c \land (i+1) B_r > j B_c$): Partial causal mask applied in thread registers.
       * **Super-diagonal Tiles** ($ (i+1) B_r \le j B_c $): Completely skipped ($0$ FLOPs, $0$ SRAM loads).
  2. **Causal FLOPs Halving Theorem**:
     $$\text{Total Active Tiles} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{N}{B} \right) \left( \frac{N}{B} + 1 \right) \approx \frac{N^2}{2 B^2}$$
     $$\text{Causal FLOPs} = 2 N^2 d \quad (50\% \text{ reduction vs non-causal attention})$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that super-diagonal tiles with $j > i \frac{B_r}{B_c}$ have attention weight $P^{(j)} = \mathbf{0}$, proving that skipping them introduces zero numerical perturbation.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the exact active tile formula $N_{\text{active}} = \frac{1}{2} K (K + 1)$ where $K = N / B$, and show that as $K \to \infty$, the fraction of skipped computation approaches exactly $50\%$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For sequence length $N = 8192$ and block size $B = 128$ ($K = 64$ blocks), compute the exact number of active tiles ($N_{\text{active}} = 2{,}080$) vs full grid ($4{,}096$), saving $49.2\%$ of GPU compute cycles.
