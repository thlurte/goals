### 🔹 Part 1: Advanced Theorems & Derivations (Thursday Syllabus)
* **Book / Topic**: *Computational Optimal Transport* — Gabriel Peyré & Marco Cuturi Ch 2 (*Semi-Relaxed Transport & MaxSim Equivalence*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **MaxSim as Semi-Relaxed Optimal Transport**:
     * Let $C_{ij} = 1 - \mathbf{q}_i^T \mathbf{d}_j$ be the transport cost between query token $i$ and document visual token $j$.
     * Consider the asymmetric transport problem:
       $$\min_{P \ge 0} \sum_{i=1}^{|Q|} \sum_{j=1}^{|D|} P_{ij} C_{ij} \quad \text{subject to} \quad \sum_{j=1}^{|D|} P_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i$$
     * **Exact Equivalence Theorem**: The optimal transport plan is $P^*_{ij} = \mathbf{1}_{\{ j = \arg\max_k \mathbf{q}_i^T \mathbf{d}_k \}}$, yielding:
       $$\operatorname{Score}_{\text{MaxSim}}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{D}) = |Q| - \min_P \langle P, C \rangle = \sum_{i=1}^{|Q|} \max_{j=1}^{|D|} \left( \mathbf{q}_i^T \mathbf{d}_j \right)$$
  2. **Lipschitz Continuity of MaxSim**:
     * For any two query token matrices $\mathbf{Q}_1, \mathbf{Q}_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{|Q| \times d}$ and unit-norm document tokens:
       $$\left| \operatorname{Score}(\mathbf{Q}_1, \mathbf{D}) - \operatorname{Score}(\mathbf{Q}_2, \mathbf{D}) \right| \le \sum_{i=1}^{|Q|} \|\mathbf{q}_{1,i} - \mathbf{q}_{2,i}\|_2$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that the semi-relaxed optimal transport problem decomposes into $|Q|$ independent linear programs, each minimized by the extreme point $P_{ij}^* = 1$ at the maximum inner product.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the MaxSim Lipschitz continuity bound step-by-step using the triangle inequality on inner products.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For $|Q| = 32$ query tokens, $|D| = 1030$ visual tokens, and $d = 128$, compute total GEMM computation volume ($8.438\text{ MFLOPs}$) and calculate theoretical execution time on a $120\text{ TFLOPS}$ GPU ($t_{\text{GEMM}} \approx 70.3\text{ ns}$ per page).

### 🔹 Part 2: Core Proofs & Problem Sets (Friday Syllabus)
* **Book / Topic**: *Metric Embeddings & Retrieval Bounds in Multi-Vector Spaces* — Piotr Indyk & Jiří Matoušek
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Two-Stage Multi-Vector Recall Bound**:
     * Let $\mathcal{D}^*_k$ be the exact top-$k$ documents under MaxSim.
     * With $C$ centroids per document and Voronoi cluster variance $\sigma_c^2$, the probability that a true top-$k$ document is retained in coarse candidate list $K$ satisfies:
       $$P(\mathcal{D}^*_k \subseteq \text{Candidates}_K) \ge 1 - k \cdot \exp\left( -\frac{C \cdot \Delta^2}{2 \sigma_c^2} \right)$$
     * Where $\Delta = \operatorname{Score}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{D}^*) - \operatorname{Score}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{D}_{k+1})$ is the retrieval margin.
  2. **Pareto Optimal Candidate Threshold**:
     * Total search latency $T(K) = T_{\text{coarse}} + K \cdot T_{\text{MaxSim\_page}}$.
     * Setting $K^* = \frac{2 \sigma_c^2}{\Delta^2} \ln(k / \delta)$ achieves recall $\ge 1 - \delta$ with minimal compute.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Derive the coarse candidate recall bound using the union bound and Sub-Gaussian concentration on inner product perturbations.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the optimal candidate count $K^*$ that minimizes total latency subject to target recall $R \ge 0.98$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For $N = 100{,}000$ documents ($T_{\text{coarse}} = 0.6\text{ ms}, T_{\text{MaxSim}} = 75\text{ ns}$ per page), compute total search latency for $K = 200$ candidates ($t_{\text{total}} = 0.615\text{ ms}$), achieving $>99.1\%$ recall@10.
