## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book / Paper**: *Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss & TurboQuant: Rate-Distortion Bounds for Attention Key-Value Caching* — Amir Zandieh et al. (2024)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Orthogonal Dimensions of KV-Cache Compression**:
     * Total Memory Footprint:
       $$\text{Memory} = B_{\text{active}} \times L \times 2 H_{kv} \times d \times b \text{ bits}$$
     * **Paging (vLLM)**: Optimizes allocation geometry ($B_{\text{active}} \times L$), eliminating fragmentation $W \to 0$.
     * **Quantization (TurboQuant)**: Optimizes representation bitwidth ($b: 16 \to 3$ bits).
     * **Multiplicative Scaling Theorem**:
       $$\text{Total VRAM Savings} = S_{\text{paging}} \times S_{\text{quant}} = \left(\frac{L_{\max}}{\mathbb{E}[L]}\right) \times \left(\frac{16}{b}\right) \approx 4 \times 5.33 \approx 21.3\times$$
  2. **Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss (QJL) Unbiased Estimator**:
     * Under random orthogonal rotation $\Pi \in \mathcal{O}(d)$ and $b$-bit scalar quantizer $\mathcal{Q}_b$:
       $$\mathbb{E}_{\Pi}\left[ \langle \mathbf{q}, \Pi^T \mathcal{Q}_b(\Pi \mathbf{k}) \rangle \right] = \langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{k} \rangle$$
       $$\operatorname{Var}\left( \langle \mathbf{q}, \Pi^T \mathcal{Q}_b(\Pi \mathbf{k}) \rangle \right) \le \frac{C_b}{d} \|\mathbf{q}\|_2^2 \|\mathbf{k}\|_2^2 \quad \left(C_b \approx \frac{\pi}{2} 2^{-2b}\right)$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove the QJL unbiasedness theorem: Show that $\mathbb{E}[\Pi^T \mathcal{Q}_b(\Pi \mathbf{k})] = \mathbf{k}$ over the Haar measure on orthogonal matrices.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the variance decay $\mathcal{O}(1/d)$: Show that random orthogonal mixing disperses outlier coordinates across all $d$ dimensions, bounding maximum quantization error.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For a $128\text{k}$ context request on a 70B model ($128\text{ GB}$ baseline FP16 contiguous KV cache): calculate memory footprint under combined PagedAttention ($4\times$) + 3-bit PolarQuant ($5.33\times$), demonstrating total reduction to $6.0\text{ GB}$.

## 📖 07:30 – 08:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Book / Paper**: *TurboQuant KV Serving: In-SRAM Dequantization Pipelines in PagedAttention*
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Fused Paged-Quantized Storage Layout**:
     * Physical blocks in VRAM store 4-bit packed integer codes ($2$ elements per byte) alongside per-block FP16 scale and min offset.
     * PagedAttention kernel loads packed bytes from VRAM at $4\times$ reduced DRAM bandwidth, unpacking and converting to FP16 directly in thread registers.
  2. **Architectural Trade-Off Analysis**:
     * Paging requires zero mathematical approximations (exact bit-level FP16 preservation).
     * Quantization trades small bounded attention variance for massive DRAM bandwidth reduction during memory-bound decoding.
