* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Contiguous Multi-Vector Storage**:
     * Pack variable-length visual tokens in contiguous VRAM/DRAM buffers indexed by `uint64_t doc_offsets[N+1]` (CSR structure) for memory-coalesced streaming.
  2. **Two-Stage Retrieval Cascade**:
     * Stage 1: Coarse candidate selection via centroid routing index on CPU/GPU ($\approx 0.8\text{ ms}$ over 100K documents $\to 128$ candidates).
     * Stage 2: Exact fused GPU MaxSim over top-128 candidate token matrices ($\approx 95\ \mu\text{s}$).
