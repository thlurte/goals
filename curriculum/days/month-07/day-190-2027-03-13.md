### 🔹 Part 2: Properties & Geometric Symmetries (Tuesday Syllabus)
* **Book / Topic**: *Multi-Objective Optimization & Pareto Efficiency in Metric Search* (Kalyanmoy Deb Ch 2) & *Whiteboard Rehearsal: Proofs 3 & 4*
* **References**: [`roadmap.md Part 5 §3 & §4`](../../roadmap.md#part-5-master-appendix--the-8-canonical-proofs-of-vector-search--ai-systems)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Pareto Dominance in Vector Retrieval**:
     * A configuration $\theta_1 = (\text{Recall}_1, \text{QPS}_1)$ strictly dominates $\theta_2 = (\text{Recall}_2, \text{QPS}_2)$ ($\theta_1 \succ \theta_2$) iff:
       $$\text{Recall}_1 \ge \text{Recall}_2 \land \text{QPS}_1 \ge \text{QPS}_2 \land (\text{Recall}_1 > \text{Recall}_2 \lor \text{QPS}_1 > \text{QPS}_2)$$
     * **Pareto Frontier $\mathcal{P}^*$**: The boundary set of non-dominated operating points.
  2. **Hypervolume Indicator Metric**:
     $$\operatorname{HV}(\mathcal{P}, \mathbf{r}_{\text{ref}}) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^2} \mathbf{1}_{\{ \mathbf{z} \ge \mathbf{r}_{\text{ref}} \land \exists \theta \in \mathcal{P}: \theta \succeq \mathbf{z} \}} d\mathbf{z}$$
     * **Optimality Theorem**: Maximizing the Hypervolume indicator is strictly congruent with achieving the optimal continuous trade-off between index accuracy and query throughput.
  3. **Canonical Proof 3: Scaled Dot-Product Attention Backward Pass** ([`roadmap.md#3`](../../roadmap.md#3-scaled-dot-product-attention-backward-pass)):
     * Matrix trace differentials $d\mathcal{L} = \operatorname{Tr}\left(\left(\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial O}\right)^T dO\right)$.
     * Derivation of $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial V} = P^T \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial O}$, $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial Q} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial S} K$, and $\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial K} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}}\left(\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial S}\right)^T Q$.
  4. **Canonical Proof 4: FlashAttention Online Softmax Numeric Invariant** ([`roadmap.md#4`](../../roadmap.md#4-flashattention-online-softmax-numeric-invariant)):
     * Exact online update equations: $m^{\text{new}} = \max(m^{(1)}, m^{(2)})$ and $\ell^{\text{new}} = \ell^{(1)} e^{m^{(1)} - m^{\text{new}}} + \ell^{(2)} e^{m^{(2)} - m^{\text{new}}}$.
     * Proof that unnormalized output accumulator update $U^{\text{new}} = e^{m^{(1)} - m^{\text{new}}} U^{(1)} + e^{m^{(2)} - m^{\text{new}}} U^{(2)}$ computes mathematically identical results with zero HBM write traffic.

### 🔹 Part 3: Core Theorems & Derivations (Wednesday Syllabus)
* **Book / Topic**: *Hybrid Retrieval Theory: Reciprocal Rank Fusion & Convex Score Spaces* (Gordon V. Cormack et al.) & *Whiteboard Rehearsal: Proofs 5 & 6*
* **References**: [`roadmap.md Part 5 §5 & §6`](../../roadmap.md#part-5-master-appendix--the-8-canonical-proofs-of-vector-search--ai-systems)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) Formulation**:
     * Given $M$ independent retrieval rankings (e.g. Dense HNSW, Multi-Vector MaxSim, Sparse BM25):
       $$\operatorname{Score}_{\text{RRF}}(d) = \sum_{m=1}^M \frac{1}{k_0 + r_m(d)}$$
     * Where $r_m(d) \in \{1, 2, \dots, K\}$ is the rank of document $d$ in system $m$, and $k_0 \approx 60$ is the rank damping constant.
  2. **Monotonic Invariance Theorem**:
     * For any strictly monotonic score transformations $g_m: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ with $g_m'(s) > 0$, the RRF score is strictly invariant:
       $$\operatorname{RRF}(\{g_m(S_m)\}_{m=1}^M) \equiv \operatorname{RRF}(\{S_m\}_{m=1}^M)$$
     * Completely eliminates cross-model calibration and score distribution mismatch between uncalibrated cosine logits and BM25 scores.
  3. **Canonical Proof 5: ScaNN Directional / Anisotropic Error Decomposition** ([`roadmap.md#5`](../../roadmap.md#5-scann-directional--anisotropic-error-decomposition)):
     * Orthogonal error $\mathbb{E}[\langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{e}_\perp \rangle] = 0$ with $\operatorname{Var} = \mathcal{O}(1/D)$.
     * Parallel error $\langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{e}_\parallel \rangle = c \frac{\langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{x} \rangle}{\|\mathbf{x}\|_2^2}\|\mathbf{x}\|_2^2$ creates direct rank bias, knocking true Top-1 neighbors out. Anisotropic loss weighting $\mathcal{L} = h \|\mathbf{e}_\parallel\|^2 + \|\mathbf{e}_\perp\|^2$.
  4. **Canonical Proof 6: Kleinberg's Small-World Routing & HNSW $O(\log N)$ Traversal** ([`roadmap.md#6`](../../roadmap.md#6-kleinbergs-small-world-theorem--hnsw-logarithmic-routing)):
     * Power-law connection probability $P(u \to v) \propto d(u, v)^{-r}$. Unique scale-invariance at $r = D$ yielding bridge edge probability $\frac{\text{const}}{\ln N}$ across every octave scale $[2^j, 2^{j+1}]$.
     * Logarithmic distance halving in $\mathcal{O}(\log^2 N)$ steps, accelerated to $\mathcal{O}(\log N)$ in HNSW via explicit geometric skip-list hierarchy ($m_L = 1/\ln M$).
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove the RRF Monotonic Invariance Theorem by showing that ranking permutations $\pi_m = \operatorname{argsort}(S_m)$ are invariant under any strictly increasing bijection $g_m$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the maximum score bound $\operatorname{Score}_{\text{RRF}}(d) \le \frac{M}{k_0 + 1}$ and prove that agreeing on rank 1 across all $M$ systems produces the unique global maximum.
  - [ ] **Proof 3**: Rehearse Canonical Proofs 5 & 6 from cold memory on whiteboard without looking at notes.
  - [ ] **Problem 4**: For a hybrid search query with Dense rank $r_{\text{dense}}(d_A) = 1, r_{\text{dense}}(d_B) = 3$ and BM25 rank $r_{\text{BM25}}(d_A) = 5, r_{\text{BM25}}(d_B) = 1$, compute RRF scores with $k_0 = 60$ and determine whether document $A$ or $B$ ranks first.
