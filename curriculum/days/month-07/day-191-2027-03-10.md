## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book / Topic**: *Multi-Objective Optimization & Pareto Efficiency in Metric Search* — Kalyanmoy Deb Ch 2
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Pareto Dominance in Vector Retrieval**:
     * A configuration $\theta_1 = (\text{Recall}_1, \text{QPS}_1)$ strictly dominates $\theta_2 = (\text{Recall}_2, \text{QPS}_2)$ ($\theta_1 \succ \theta_2$) iff:
       $$\text{Recall}_1 \ge \text{Recall}_2 \land \text{QPS}_1 \ge \text{QPS}_2 \land (\text{Recall}_1 > \text{Recall}_2 \lor \text{QPS}_1 > \text{QPS}_2)$$
     * **Pareto Frontier $\mathcal{P}^*$**: The boundary set of non-dominated operating points.
  2. **Hypervolume Indicator Metric**:
     $$\operatorname{HV}(\mathcal{P}, \mathbf{r}_{\text{ref}}) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^2} \mathbf{1}_{\{ \mathbf{z} \ge \mathbf{r}_{\text{ref}} \land \exists \theta \in \mathcal{P}: \theta \succeq \mathbf{z} \}} d\mathbf{z}$$
     * **Optimality Theorem**: Maximizing the Hypervolume indicator is strictly congruent with achieving the optimal continuous trade-off between index accuracy and query throughput.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that the Pareto frontier in the loss-latency space $(1 - \text{Recall}, \text{Latency})$ is strictly monotonically non-decreasing.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the Hypervolume difference formula $\Delta \operatorname{HV} = (R - R_{\text{ref}}) \Delta \text{QPS}$ showing that throughput improvements at high recall ($R > 0.95$) dominate lower-recall regimes in total objective score.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: On SIFT1M (1M 128-d vectors), compute the Pareto points for `secan` (HNSW: $R=0.993, QPS=15{,}200$; FastScan: $R=0.932, QPS=48{,}000$; GPU-CAGRA: $R=0.995, QPS=110{,}000$) and verify domination over external baselines.
