## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book / Topic**: *Spectral Sparsification of Graphs* — Joshua Batson, Daniel A. Spielman & Nikhil Srivastava (2011)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Spectral Graph Sparsification Theorem**:
     * For any connected graph $G = (V, E)$ and approximation factor $\epsilon \in (0, 1)$, there exists a sparse subgraph $H = (V, E_H, \tilde{w})$ with at most $|E_H| = \mathcal{O}(n / \epsilon^2)$ edges such that:
       $$(1 - \epsilon) \mathbf{x}^T L_G \mathbf{x} \le \mathbf{x}^T L_H \mathbf{x} \le (1 + \epsilon) \mathbf{x}^T L_G \mathbf{x} \quad \forall \mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$$
  2. **Foster's Effective Resistance Theorem**:
     * Let $R_{\text{eff}}(u, v) = (\mathbf{e}_u - \mathbf{e}_v)^T L^+ (\mathbf{e}_u - \mathbf{e}_v)$ be the electrical effective resistance:
       $$\sum_{e = (u, v) \in E} w_e R_{\text{eff}}(u, v) = n - 1$$
     * Sampling edges with probability $p_e \propto w_e R_{\text{eff}}(e)$ guarantees exact spectral preservation of the ACORN graph while pruning $65\%$ of redundant 2-hop edges.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove Foster's Theorem by evaluating the trace $\operatorname{Tr}(L^+ L) = \operatorname{Tr}(I - \frac{1}{n}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^T) = n - 1$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that if $H$ spectrally approximates $G$ within $\epsilon$, then the second eigenvalues satisfy $(1-\epsilon)\lambda_2(G) \le \lambda_2(H) \le (1+\epsilon)\lambda_2(G)$, preserving search conductance $\phi(H) \ge \frac{1-\epsilon}{2}\lambda_2(G)$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For an ACORN predicate graph with $N = 100{,}000$ vertices and 2-hop expanded degree $D = 120$ ($6 \times 10^6$ edges), compute the target sparsified edge count for $\epsilon = 0.25$ ($|E_H| \approx 1.6 \times 10^6$ edges, saving $73.3\%$ RAM).
