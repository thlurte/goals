## 📖 05:30 – 06:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Book / Paper**: *Orca & vLLM: Iteration-Level Continuous Batching Architecture*
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Zero-Bubble Continuous Batching**:
     * Traditional static batching stalls an entire batch until the longest request finishes.
     * Iteration-level scheduling executes one token step per iteration; completed sequences evict their `BlockTable` pages immediately, permitting new requests to join without bubble latency.
  2. **DRAM Bandwidth Amortization**:
     * At high concurrency ($B \ge 128$), reading the 16 GB model weights is amortized across 128 tokens, lifting memory bandwidth efficiency to $>90\%$.
