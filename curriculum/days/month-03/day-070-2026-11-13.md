## 📖 05:30 – 06:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Advanced Systems Reference**: * [Donald E. Knuth, *The Art of Computer Programming, Vol 3: Sorting and Searching*](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/taocp.html) — **§6.4 & §6.5** (*Cache-Conscious Robin Hood Hashing & Multi-Dimensional Inverted Files*)
* **Paper**: *"TurboQuant: Redefining AI Efficiency with Extreme Vector Compression"* (Google Research, 2024)
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Polar Coordinate Quantization (PolarQuant)**:
     * Deconstructs vector $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ into magnitude $r = \|\mathbf{x}\|_2$ and unit direction $\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{x} / r$.
     * Direction $\mathbf{u}$ is compressed using 2-bit polar angular sector quantization.
  2. **Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss (QJL) Residual Correction**:
     * Appends a 1-bit random sketch $\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf{S}\mathbf{x})$ where $\mathbf{S} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times d}$.
     * Provides an unbiased estimator for inner products with provably bounded variance, recovering $>98\%$ top-1 accuracy at $2.5\text{ bits/dimension}$.
