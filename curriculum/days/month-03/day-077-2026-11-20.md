## ✍️ 06:30 – 08:30 | Morning Builder Track: Technical Essay / Lab-Note Drafting (120 min)
* **Essay Number**: **Essay 11**
* **Title**: *"Eigenvalues, Spectral Decompositions, and LSM Storage Engines: Write-Ahead Logs and Vector Compaction"*
* **Target File**: `~/personal/goals/essays/essay_11.md`
* **5-Part Standard Structure**:
  1. **Mathematical Foundation**:
     * Determinants, characteristic polynomials, and matrix diagonalization $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{S}\mathbf{\Lambda}\mathbf{S}^{-1}$.
     * The Spectral Theorem $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{Q}\mathbf{\Lambda}\mathbf{Q}^T$ and symmetric positive definiteness tests.
     * Cholesky factorization $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^T$ as an energy minimizer.
  2. **The Systems Bottleneck**:
     * The dynamic index dilemma: Mutable in-place graph updates cause random I/O thrashing and lock contention.
     * Log-Structured Merge (LSM) storage: Sequential disk streaming and memory-mapped immutable segments.
  3. **The Engine Architecture**:
     * `secan` LSM design: CRC32-checked WAL, dynamic HNSW `MemTable`, columnar `SegmentFlusher`, and asynchronous compactor.
  4. **Empirical Benchmarks**:
     * Ingestion throughput: $12{,}500\text{ inserts/sec}$ sustained under concurrent 100 QPS search traffic; crash recovery replaying $100{,}000$ vectors in $<85\text{ ms}$.
  5. **Key Takeaway**:
     * High-throughput AI systems decouple ingestion from querying: append-only logging guarantees durability, while background compaction preserves low query latency.
