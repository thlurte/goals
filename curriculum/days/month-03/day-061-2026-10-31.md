## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Linear Algebra* — Gilbert Strang
* **Sections**: §2.6 (*The Fundamental Theorem of Linear Algebra Part 2: Orthogonal Complements*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Fundamental Theorem of Linear Algebra (Part 2: Orthogonality)**:
     $$N(\mathbf{A}) = C(\mathbf{A}^T)^\perp \quad \text{in } \mathbb{R}^n$$
     $$N(\mathbf{A}^T) = C(\mathbf{A})^\perp \quad \text{in } \mathbb{R}^m$$
     * Every vector in the nullspace is perpendicular to every vector in the row space.
     * Every vector in the left nullspace is perpendicular to every vector in the column space.
  2. **Direct Sum Decomposition of Ambient Spaces**:
     $$\mathbb{R}^n = C(\mathbf{A}^T) \oplus N(\mathbf{A}), \quad \mathbb{R}^m = C(\mathbf{A}) \oplus N(\mathbf{A}^T)$$
     * Any vector $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ has a unique orthogonal decomposition $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{x}_{\text{row}} + \mathbf{x}_{\text{null}}$ where $\mathbf{x}_{\text{row}} \in C(\mathbf{A}^T)$ and $\mathbf{x}_{\text{null}} \in N(\mathbf{A})$.
     * Mapping property: $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_{\text{row}} + \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_{\text{row}}$, establishing a bijection between the row space and column space!
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:

  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that $V \cap V^\perp = \{\mathbf{0}\}$ for any linear subspace $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For matrix $\mathbf{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$, uniquely decompose vector $\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$ into $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{x}_r + \mathbf{x}_n$ where $\mathbf{x}_r \in C(\mathbf{A}^T)$ and $\mathbf{x}_n \in N(\mathbf{A})$.
