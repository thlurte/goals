## 🌅 06:00 – 07:30 | Pure Mathematics (90 min)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Linear Algebra* — Gilbert Strang
* **Sections**: §6.7 (*Matrix Norms, Condition Numbers $\kappa(\mathbf{A})$ & System Sensitivity*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **Induced Matrix Norms**:
     * **$L_1$ Column Norm**: $\|\mathbf{A}\|_1 = \max_{1 \le j \le n} \sum_{i=1}^m |a_{ij}|$.
     * **$L_\infty$ Row Norm**: $\|\mathbf{A}\|_\infty = \max_{1 \le i \le m} \sum_{j=1}^n |a_{ij}|$.
     * **$L_2$ Spectral Norm**: $\|\mathbf{A}\|_2 = \max_{\|\mathbf{x}\|_2=1} \|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}\|_2 = \sigma_1(\mathbf{A})$.
     * **Frobenius Norm**: $\|\mathbf{A}\|_F = \sqrt{\operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{A}^T \mathbf{A})} = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^r \sigma_i^2}$.
  2. **The Condition Number $\kappa(\mathbf{A})$**:
     $$\kappa(\mathbf{A}) = \|\mathbf{A}\|_2 \|\mathbf{A}^{-1}\|_2 = \frac{\sigma_{\max}(\mathbf{A})}{\sigma_{\min}(\mathbf{A})} = \frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_n}$$
  3. **Relative Error Amplification Bound**:
     * In linear systems $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$, a perturbation $\delta \mathbf{b}$ induces a solution error $\delta \mathbf{x}$:
       $$\frac{\|\delta \mathbf{x}\|}{\|\mathbf{x}\|} \le \kappa(\mathbf{A}) \frac{\|\delta \mathbf{b}\|}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}$$
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that $\|\mathbf{A}\|_2 = \sigma_1$ using the SVD factorization $\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{U}\mathbf{\Sigma}\mathbf{V}^T$ and orthogonal invariance $\|\mathbf{U}\mathbf{y}\|_2 = \|\mathbf{y}\|_2$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Derive the error bound $\frac{\|\delta \mathbf{x}\|}{\|\mathbf{x}\|} \le \kappa(\mathbf{A})\frac{\|\delta \mathbf{b}\|}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}$ by combining $\|\mathbf{b}\| \le \|\mathbf{A}\| \|\mathbf{x}\|$ with $\|\delta \mathbf{x}\| \le \|\mathbf{A}^{-1}\| \|\delta \mathbf{b}\|$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For matrix $\mathbf{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1.0001 \end{pmatrix}$, compute its exact condition number $\kappa(\mathbf{A}) \approx 4 \times 10^4$. Solve $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{x}+\delta \mathbf{x}) = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 2.0001 \end{pmatrix}$, demonstrating massive solution drift.
