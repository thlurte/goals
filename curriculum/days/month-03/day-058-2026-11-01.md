### 🔹 Part 1: Advanced Theorems & Derivations (Thursday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Linear Algebra* — Gilbert Strang
* **Sections**: §2.5–2.6 (*The Four Fundamental Subspaces & Fundamental Theorem of Linear Algebra Part 1*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Four Fundamental Subspaces of Matrix $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$**:
     | Subspace | Notation | Ambient Space | Dimension |
     |:---|:---|:---|:---|
     | **Column Space** | $C(\mathbf{A})$ | $\mathbb{R}^m$ | $r$ (rank) |
     | **Nullspace** | $N(\mathbf{A})$ | $\mathbb{R}^n$ | $n - r$ |
     | **Row Space** | $C(\mathbf{A}^T)$ | $\mathbb{R}^n$ | $r$ (rank) |
     | **Left Nullspace** | $N(\mathbf{A}^T)$ | $\mathbb{R}^m$ | $m - r$ |
  2. **The Fundamental Theorem of Linear Algebra (Part 1)**:
     * Row rank equals column rank: $\operatorname{dim}(C(\mathbf{A}^T)) = \operatorname{dim}(C(\mathbf{A})) = r$.
     * The dimensions of the row space and nullspace sum to $n$; the dimensions of the column space and left nullspace sum to $m$.
  3. **Left Nullspace Mechanics**:
     * $\mathbf{y} \in N(\mathbf{A}^T) \iff \mathbf{A}^T \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{0} \iff \mathbf{y}^T \mathbf{A} = \mathbf{0}^T$.
     * Row operations that zero out rows during elimination produce the exact basis vectors for the left nullspace.
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove that row rank equals column rank ($\operatorname{dim} C(\mathbf{A}^T) = \operatorname{dim} C(\mathbf{A}) = r$) by showing that row operations preserve the row space and the linear independence of pivot columns.
  - [ ] **Problem 2**: For matrix $\mathbf{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 3 & 3 & 2 \\ 2 & 6 & 9 & 7 \\ -1 & -3 & 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$, compute explicit bases and dimensions for all four fundamental subspaces: $C(\mathbf{A}), N(\mathbf{A}), C(\mathbf{A}^T), N(\mathbf{A}^T)$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For an $m \times n$ matrix with $m > n$ and full column rank $r = n$, determine the dimensions of all four fundamental subspaces and explain why $N(\mathbf{A}) = \{\mathbf{0}\}$.

### 🔹 Part 2: Core Proofs & Problem Sets (Friday Syllabus)
* **Book**: *Introduction to Linear Algebra* — Gilbert Strang
* **Sections**: §2.6 (*The Fundamental Theorem of Linear Algebra Part 2: Orthogonal Complements*)
* **Theorems & Mathematical Concepts**:
  1. **The Fundamental Theorem of Linear Algebra (Part 2: Orthogonality)**:
     $$N(\mathbf{A}) = C(\mathbf{A}^T)^\perp \quad \text{in } \mathbb{R}^n$$
     $$N(\mathbf{A}^T) = C(\mathbf{A})^\perp \quad \text{in } \mathbb{R}^m$$
     * Every vector in the nullspace is perpendicular to every vector in the row space.
     * Every vector in the left nullspace is perpendicular to every vector in the column space.
  2. **Direct Sum Decomposition of Ambient Spaces**:
     $$\mathbb{R}^n = C(\mathbf{A}^T) \oplus N(\mathbf{A}), \quad \mathbb{R}^m = C(\mathbf{A}) \oplus N(\mathbf{A}^T)$$
     * Any vector $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ has a unique orthogonal decomposition $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{x}_{\text{row}} + \mathbf{x}_{\text{null}}$ where $\mathbf{x}_{\text{row}} \in C(\mathbf{A}^T)$ and $\mathbf{x}_{\text{null}} \in N(\mathbf{A})$.
     * Mapping property: $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_{\text{row}} + \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}_{\text{row}}$, establishing a bijection between the row space and column space!
* **Pencil & Paper Problem Set**:
  - [ ] **Proof 1**: Prove $N(\mathbf{A}) \perp C(\mathbf{A}^T)$ directly: show that if $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{0}$, then $\mathbf{x}$ is orthogonal to every linear combination of rows $\mathbf{y} = \mathbf{A}^T \mathbf{c}$.
  - [ ] **Proof 2**: Prove that $V \cap V^\perp = \{\mathbf{0}\}$ for any linear subspace $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$.
  - [ ] **Problem 3**: For matrix $\mathbf{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$, uniquely decompose vector $\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$ into $\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{x}_r + \mathbf{x}_n$ where $\mathbf{x}_r \in C(\mathbf{A}^T)$ and $\mathbf{x}_n \in N(\mathbf{A})$.
