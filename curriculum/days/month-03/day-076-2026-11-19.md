## 📖 05:30 – 06:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Book**: *High-Performance Asynchronous C++* — Marek Ellison (Ch 6 & Ch 9)
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **Reader-Writer Concurrency for Dynamic Vector Engines**:
     * Searching while ingesting requires concurrent read access across the active `MemTable` and multiple immutable disk segments.
     * `std::shared_mutex` pattern:
       * Read queries acquire `std::shared_lock<std::shared_mutex>` (allows 100+ concurrent worker threads).
       * MemTable flusher acquires exclusive `std::unique_lock<std::shared_mutex>` for $<50\text{ ns}$ only to atomically swap pointers (`active_memtable` $\to$ `immutable_segments.push_back`).
  2. **ThreadSanitizer (`TSan`) Verification**:
     * Build with `-fsanitize=thread -g` to detect data races on memory-mapped buffer pointers and lockless sequence counters.
