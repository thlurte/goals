## 📖 05:30 – 06:30 | Systems & Architecture Reading (60 min)
* **Paper**: *PLAID: Performance-Optimized Late Interaction for Asymmetric Information Retrieval* §3–5
* **Core Microarchitectural Concepts**:
  1. **3-Stage Cascaded Filtering Architecture**:
     * Stage 1 (Centroid Inverted List Intersect): Pull candidate passages that share token centroids with the query ($1{,}000{,}000 \to 2{,}000$ candidates).
     * Stage 2 (Quantized 2-Bit SIMD MaxSim): Compute fast approximate MaxSim scores using AVX2 byte integer SIMD, dropping $80\%$ of poor candidates ($2{,}000 \to 400$ candidates).
     * Stage 3 (FP32 Decompressed MaxSim): Compute exact full-precision MaxSim only on the surviving 400 candidates ($400 \to 10$ final results).
  2. **SIMD Throughput Savings**:
     * Stage 2 operates entirely out of L2 cache, eliminating $90\%$ of DRAM bandwidth consumption.
