# Evening & Weekend Intellectual Reading Plan (Sep 2026 – Mar 2027)

A disciplined, restorative 28-week reading curriculum designed for the post-work evening decompression sanctuary (18:30–20:00) and relaxed weekend afternoons.

---

## 🏛️ The Three Reading Pillars

| Pillar | Focus & Domain | Master Works |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **I. Soul, Morality & Craft** | Character-driven literature, craftsmanship, and human nature | *Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance* (Pirsig)<br>*The Brothers Karamazov* (Dostoevsky)<br>*The Master and Margarita* (Bulgakov) |

| **III. The Physical Cosmos & Geometry** | Visual mathematical physics, geometry, spacetime, and quantum reality | *The Road to Reality: A Complete Guide to the Laws of the Universe* (Sir Roger Penrose) |

## 💡 Practical Rules to Guarantee Success
1. **Never read behind a glowing monitor screen**: Always use a physical book or warm E-ink paper.
2. **Keep a pencil**: Underline no more than 1 sentence per 2 pages. Write 2-word marginalia.
3. **Pirsig Rule**: Target 15–20 pages per day. If a section is profound, pause and think; do not rush.
4. **Penrose Rule**: On Sundays, study the diagrams. If the equations get thorny, skip to the text and keep walking.
