## 1. Executive Abstract & Falsifiable Question

**Core Falsifiable Question**:
> *Does decomposing quantization error into parallel component $\mathbf{e}_\parallel$ and orthogonal component $\mathbf{e}_\perp$ with an anisotropic penalty weight $h \ge 5.0$ preserve $>95\%$ Top-1 Maximum Inner Product Search (MIPS) recall on anisotropic $768\text{-D}$ dense embeddings while compressing vectors by $32\times$ (down to 16–32 bytes/target)?*

---

## 2. Mathematical Foundations

Given query $\mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ and dataset vector $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ with quantized representation $\tilde{\mathbf{x}} = \mathbf{x} + \mathbf{e}$:
$$\langle \mathbf{q}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}} \rangle = \langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{x} \rangle + \langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{e}_\parallel \rangle + \langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{e}_\perp \rangle$$

1. **Orthogonal Error Asymptotics**:
   * In high dimensions, $\mathbf{q}$ is near-orthogonal to $\mathbf{e}_\perp$.
   $$\mathbb{E}[\langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{e}_\perp \rangle] = 0, \quad \operatorname{Var}(\langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{e}_\perp \rangle) = \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{D}\right)$$
2. **Parallel Error Distortion**:
   * Parallel error directly alters the inner product magnitude, causing true Top-1 nearest neighbors to drop out of retrieval candidate pools:
   $$\langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{e}_\parallel \rangle = c \cdot \langle \mathbf{q}, \mathbf{x} \rangle$$
3. **Anisotropic Objective**:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{aniso}}(\mathbf{x}, \tilde{\mathbf{x}}) = h \|\mathbf{e}_\parallel\|^2 + \|\mathbf{e}_\perp\|^2, \quad h = 1 + (D-1)\left(1 - \frac{1}{2\eta}\right)$$

## 3. Experimental Protocol & Datasets
* **Datasets**: Real 768-D dense embeddings generated by transformer models (BERT, BGE-Large, Contriever) exhibiting measurable hubness ($S_{N_k} > 1.8$).
* **Quantization Variants**:
  1. Standard MSE Product Quantization ($k=256$, $M=96$).
  2. Optimized Product Quantization (OPQ / PCA rotation).
  3. ScaNN Anisotropic Vector Quantization ($h=5.0$).
  4. 1-bit Binary Quantization (BQ) with SIMD Hamming Popcount.

---

## 4. Key Empirical Results & Profiler Evidence
* **Bandwidth Reduction**: Vector payload compressed from **$3{,}072	ext{ bytes}$** to **$96	ext{ bytes}$** (4-bit FastScan) and **$96	ext{ bits}$ (12 bytes)** (BQ).
* **MIPS Recall Retention**: ScaNN anisotropic loss recovers **$>96.2\%$ Recall@10**, whereas MSE PQ collapses to **$74.1\%$** under identical bit budgets on anisotropic cones.
